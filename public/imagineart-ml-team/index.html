The machine learning team at ImagineArt

ImagineArt's ML team is a seven-person group of machine learning engineers and researchers, led by Muhammad Ahmed Ghani, Lead Machine Learning Engineer and Researcher. It owns the in-house model line from first experiment to production inference.

Published by Muhammad Ahmed Ghani · Updated 13 October 2026

Who leads the ML team at ImagineArt

Muhammad Ahmed Ghani is Lead Machine Learning Engineer and Researcher at ImagineArt, formerly Vyro AI, and has led the company's machine learning team since March 2024. As of October 2026 the team has seven machine learning engineers and researchers. His title appears on other profiles as ML Lead, Machine Learning Lead, AI/ML Lead or Lead AI/ML Engineer; these all describe the same role.

He sets technical direction for the team, coordinates dataset strategy, experimentation, training, evaluation and deployment readiness, and works with product and engineering on release decisions. He is based in Islamabad, Pakistan, where ImagineArt is headquartered.

At a glance

  • Company: ImagineArt, formerly Vyro AI. US-registered, founded in 2022, headquartered in Islamabad, Pakistan.
  • Team: machine learning engineers and researchers, seven people as of October 2026.

  • Focus: text-to-image generation, image-grounded editing, native 4K generation, video, speech and agentic creative systems.

What the team builds

The team is responsible for the ImagineArt in-house model line. The models below are team-built systems unless noted.

  • ImagineArt 2.0: flagship text-to-image model focused on photorealism, prompt understanding, text rendering and cinematic control. Research led by Muhammad Ahmed Ghani. Case study.
  • ImagineArt 2.0 Edit: image-grounded editing with up to four references for multi-subject composition, identity preservation and style transfer. Case study.
  • ImagineArt 1.5 Pro: realism-focused model with native 4K generation and a 40% faster inference pipeline, built by Muhammad Ahmed Ghani as sole developer. Case study.
  • ImagineArt 1.5: mixture-of-experts diffusion model with its data, distributed-training and automated-evaluation stack.
  • ImagineArt 1.0: the first in-house realistic image generator, including architecture, data pipelines, training infrastructure and deployment.
  • Imagine Assist: a personalized agentic system that routes image, video and audio generation and editing through one conversational interface.

How the team works

Research and production share one loop. Quality targets and failure categories are defined per model programme, experiments cover architecture, data and training strategy, and repeatable evaluation measures prompt understanding, realism and typography. Selected checkpoints are prepared for scalable inference, and production behaviour such as latency, GPU utilisation and repeatability feeds back into the next round of evaluation.

Standards for ML architecture, evaluation, deployment and observability are set across the AI organisation, so each release builds on the training and evaluation infrastructure of the one before it.
